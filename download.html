My Car

Diagnose, watch and talk to your car. By Garage on Wheels.

⬇ Download for Android
Android app · 4 MB
  1. Open the downloaded MyCar.apk and tap Install.
  2. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser, then tap Install again.
  3. Open My Car, tap Sign up and enter the code from your email.
  4. Plug a Bluetooth OBD adapter into your car and connect.
